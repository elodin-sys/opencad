# ECR-2: 3 mm minimum shell thickness

Raise the minimum shell thickness from 2 mm to 3 mm: every wall, floor or lid panel thinner than 3 mm grows away
from the interior (outward, downward for the floor, upward for the lid ceiling) until it is 3 mm; panels already
3 mm or thicker do not change. The interior stays exactly where it is: the board keep-outs, the 0.5 mm edge band,
the plug envelopes, the standoff datums and pilot holes, the internal headroom and the lid/base rim mating are all
preserved, and no opening is enlarged or moved. The standoff boss annuli (1.85 mm) remain the accepted exception.
Update the acceptance envelope, the affected face levels and dimensions, and the volume and centre-of-mass targets.
