# rpi5-enclosure — design brief

## 1. Intent and configuration

Design a two-piece FFF-printed enclosure (base and removable lid, two separate solids in one STEP) for a Raspberry Pi 5 to the attached requirements document, in the board frame it defines (x along 85, y along 56, PCB bottom at z = 0). The board must fit with 0.5 mm edge clearance and 20 mm headroom above the PCB; four standoffs meet the PCB bottom at the Ø2.7 mounting holes (3.5,3.5) (61.5,3.5) (3.5,52.5) (61.5,52.5) with Ø2.5 x 5 pilot holes; every listed cable-plug envelope (USB-C, 2x micro-HDMI on y = 0; 2x USB-A stacks and RJ45 on x = 85; micro-SD under x = 0) passes through its wall opening; the lid closes on the base rim with zero interference and is held by four Ø3 through holes into Ø2.5 corner-post pilots; walls and floor >= 2 mm (5th percentile of inward ray casts; the Ø6.2 standoff bosses' 1.85 mm annuli are the accepted exception). Declare the base and lid as acceptance solids by a contained point, the keep-outs and plug envelopes as acceptance hardware with no-interference clearances, the standoff faces and pilot holes as faces and cylinders pinned by interfaces, and the wall minimum as a p05 thickness check. Headroom, wall thickness and standoff height are top-level parameters; the headroom parameter gets a 10-value regeneration sweep (15..35, every value must build) and one infeasible negative value guarded by an assert.

This is a new prototype enclosure, not a reconstruction of an enclosure shown in the board drawing. Deliver two connected solids, `base` and `lid`, in one assembled STEP in the board frame. Do not export the PCB, components, plugs or screws. The required literal top-level parameters include `headroom`, `wallThickness` and `standoffHeight`. The selected nominal design uses a straight tray, four integral cylindrical standoffs, four external square corner posts, seven open-to-rim notches and a flat screw-held lid. Its envelope is 102 × 73 × 33 mm. Open questions identify the design choices that the sources leave free; none changes a stated requirement.

## 4. Coordinate frame, scale, and measurement method

Origin is the virtual intersection of the PCB’s x=0 and y=0 straight edges on its bottom face, nearest USB-C. +x runs right along the 85 mm edge to USB-A/RJ45, +y runs upward along 56 mm toward GPIO, and +z points out of the component side. The PCB occupies z=0..1.6. Datum A is the four seats at z=0; B and C are the x=0 and y=0 board planes. Datum D is the nominal rim/lid pair z=21.55/21.6 and moves with headroom. The rounded board corner is not the origin.

The supplied PNG is 2573 × 1819 native pixels. Pixel coordinates below use its upper-left as (0,0), u right and v down; do not use the scaled chat preview. Straight plan-outline black lines are at u≈407.5 and 1144.5, v≈412.5 and 897.5. Read the red 85 and 56 dimension lines as the scale anchors, giving sx=(1144.5−407.5)/85=8.670588 px/mm and sy=(897.5−412.5)/56=8.660714 px/mm. Board mapping is x=(u−407.5)/sx, y=(897.5−v)/sy. The 0.114% scale difference is raster/line-picking uncertainty, not a design skew; no perspective correction is justified. Use ±3 px, approximately ±0.35 mm, for independent image checks; printed dimensions and text remain the governing nominal values.

Use these symbols for the analytic construction: L=boardLength, W=boardWidth, c=edgeClearance, w=wallThickness, h=standoffHeight, H=headroom, t=pcbThickness, s=cornerPostWidth, g=closureGap, l=lidThickness, d=pilotDepth, rb=standoffDiameter/2, rp=pilotDiameter/2, rl=lidHoleDiameter/2. Local computed expressions must follow edits; do not freeze derived elevations or insert equality assertions between independent parameters.

Footprint O is the union of the main outer rectangle R=[−c−w,L+c+w] × [−c−w,W+c+w] and four s × s squares. Square centers are the combinations x={−c−s/2,L+c+s/2}, y={−c−s/2,W+c+s/2}: (−4.5,−4.5), (89.5,−4.5), (−4.5,60.5), (89.5,60.5). Each square overlaps R by w² and touches the cavity corner only at its boundary; the corner posts never occupy the PCB footprint. Their combined footprint remains connected. The cavity is I=[−c,L+c] × [−c,W+c].

Ao=area(O)=(L+2(c+w))(W+2(c+w))+4(s²−w²)=5844.4 mm²; Ai=area(I)=(L+2c)(W+2c)=4902 mm²; Ar=Ao−Ai=942.4 mm². Outer perimeter Po=2(L+W+4(c+w))+16(s−w)=394.8 mm; inner perimeter Pi=2(L+W+4c)=286 mm. Floor top f=−h=−5; floor bottom b=−h−w=−7.4; lid underside u=t+H=21.6; rim r=u−g=21.55; lid top u+l=25.6. Base is O extruded b..r with I removed above f; add four rb disks f..0 and subtract four rp pilots −d..0. Subtract four corner-post rp pilots r−d..r. The separate lid is O extruded u..u+l minus four rl through holes. All material is upright/vertical in the base, and the lid is flat when printed inverted.

Each wall notch uses its source-box transverse limits and lower z, and extends all the way to r. Widths Wi are [13,12,12,15,15,17,12], bottom elevations bi=[1.6,1.6,1.6,1.6,1.6,1.6,−3]; sum(Wi)=96 mm. They cut only the corresponding straight wall; none intersects a corner post. Apply every original 3D plug box to the lid as well. At H=20 these lid cuts remove no material. At H=15, the USB-A boxes remove two lower edge reliefs of width 15 mm, depth 2.9 mm along x (85..87.9), and height 2 mm (16.6..18.6), leaving 2 mm of lid above. This is why lidThickness=4 and a moving rim do not block the USB openings at the bottom of the sweep. Do not clamp H upward or assert H>=20.

Total volume V=Ao*w+Ar*(r+h)−w*Σ[Wi(r−bi)]+4πrb²h−8πrp²d+(Ao−4πrl²)l. Base volume is the same expression without its final lid term. Surface areas are Sb=2Ao+Po(r+h+w)+Pi(r+h)−2Σ[Wi(r−bi)]+2wΣ(r−bi)+8πrbh+16πrpd and Sl=2(Ao−4πrl²)+Po*l+8πrl*l. These assume the declared square-corner, unfilleted open-slot design. No mass is asserted because density is unknown.

For center of mass, use signed primitive moments. Floor centroid=(L/2,W/2,−h−w/2); wall-ring centroid=(L/2,W/2,(r−h)/2). Slot-cut centroids use their connector transverse centers, wall midplanes y=−c−w/2 or x=−c−w/2 or x=L+c+w/2, and z=(r+bi)/2. Boss centroids=(mountX_i,mountY_i,−h/2); standoff pilot centroids=(mountX_i,mountY_i,−d/2); corner pilot centroids=(postX_i,postY_i,r−d/2). The symmetric four-hole lid centroid is (L/2,W/2,u+l/2). Then COM=Σ(Vj*Cj)/ΣVj, using negative volumes for cuts.

## 7. Unrepresentable or low-confidence features

The drawing warns that dimensions are approximate, components are incomplete and production data must be established independently. The board drawing alone does not establish actual cable plastic-body variation, FFF shrinkage, screw retention, service life or cooling performance. Requirements supply conservative boxes; preserve them literally. The image’s auxiliary holes, board corner radii, individual component packages, GPIO pins and side-view details are board hardware, not enclosure features to reproduce. No board solid, fillets, cosmetic text, vents or strain reliefs are included.

There is no schema-wide angular overhang or slicer simulation acceptance. No CAD deliverable is authored during this contract-drafting task; analytic targets require verification again on the later KCL exports.
