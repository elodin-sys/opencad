# voron-sb-duct — design brief

## 1. Intent and configuration

Reconstruct the Voron Stealthburner printhead front housing (Rapido V2 variant) as editable, parameterized KCL from the attached renders, section drawings and dimension sheet, in the reference frame (x symmetric about 0, back mating face y = 0 with the part extending to y = -27.4, z from 2.37 at the nozzle end to 61.5 at the top). The interior matters most: two mirrored closed part-cooling ducts from the top fan inlets down to the forked nozzle outlets (about 8 x 19 mm channels with ~1 mm walls, never connected to each other), the hotend cavity (about 26 x 35 mm, open to the back and the nozzle end) with the round hotend-fan opening on the front, and the screw holes. Declare the duct probe points as void probes, the sections as acceptance sections, the ducts as passages and the wall minimum as a thickness check. Cosmetic styling, text and grille are optional. One solid; export STEP and STL. Reference CAD is evaluator-only.

Deliverable configuration: editable KCL 2, length mm and angle deg; one connected material solid; deterministic STEP and a watertight STL in the same frame. This draft creates the intent bundle only. Subsequent modeling must use top-level literal constants with the exact contract parameter names, preserve their geometric dependencies, and guard infeasible edits with sanity assertions. Do not import reference geometry. Cosmetic omission is allowed; hidden geometry unsupported by these inputs is not penalised.

Source priority: the user intent controls function; the written dimension sheet controls stated coordinates and the nominal section areas; orthographic images and sections control visible shape. Keep source conflicts visible. Values quoted from the dimension sheet have provenance `stated`, even though its author measured reference CAD. There is no reference.json/profile.json ingest, and no reference CAD was accessed. The source description attributes the design to VoronDesign/Voron-Stealthburner under GPL-3.0.

## 4. Coordinate frame, scale, and measurement method

Intersection of the toolhead center plane x=0 and the back mating plane y=0 with the reference z=0 plane. This lies in the open center, 2.367 mm below the lowest material. Do not move the bbox minimum to the origin.

| axis / datum | meaning |
|---|---|
| x | Positive to the right in front.png; left/right duct routes straddle x=0. back.png uses horizontal -x. |
| y | Positive out through the back mating face toward the mating toolhead. Housing material extends into negative y, from y=0 to the front at y=-27.4. |
| z | Positive upward away from the nozzle end toward the top inlet rim; lowest material z=2.367 and top z=61.5. |
| A | Back mating patches on y=0, outward normal +y. |
| B | Center reference plane x=0, halfway between envelope extrema x=-35.534 and +35.534; local hardware and mouth details are not all symmetric. |
| C | Top inlet-rim plane z=61.5; reference z=0 is 61.5 mm below this plane, not on the part. |

Read pixels in the original files, with u right and v down from the upper-left corner. Front/back sources are 1800 x 1530; top/bottom are 1800 x 812. Use the drawn mm grid, not the rescaled chat image. Front has x=-40 at u~176 and +40 at u~1732: 1556/80 = 19.45 px/mm, x=0 at u~954. Its z=0 is v~1370, so z=(1370-v)/19.45. Back reverses x: its horizontal coordinate is -x. Top/bottom use u=954 for x=0 and 95.25 px per 5 mm, or 19.05 px/mm. Top y=0 is v~143 and y=(143-v)/19.05; bottom displays -y upward and y=(v-652)/19.05.

For section-z45.png, x=0 is u~954 and y=0 is v~180; grid pitch is about 107.35 px per 5 mm, or 21.47 px/mm. A 2-4 px stroke is only 0.1-0.2 mm, but lip shading, curved tangencies and uncertain interpolation dominate. Image parameter tolerances are therefore 0.8-1.5 mm, not sub-pixel claims. Probe contour uncertainty is budgeted at 0.75 mm and points are chosen farther inside the visible air than that. Isometric views establish adjacency and feature count only. No tessellated reference mesh was recovered from images.

## 7. Unrepresentable or low-confidence features

Finite section drawings do not determine unique NURBS surfaces or complete angular trims. Reconstruct smooth, editable transitions between the shown stations while keeping the specified walls, air probes, connectivity, screw axes and functional planes. Some cylindrical table entries are surface blends with axes outside the solid; an entry marked pin does not imply a new disconnected boss. Generic exterior facets and text/grille detail remain optional, but a functional wall or named mounting surface cannot be omitted as a cosmetic simplification.

Total reference volume, surface area, COM, 579 faces (292 planar) and 76507 render triangles are diagnostic metadata only. No exact finished-part analytic mass-property formula can be recovered from this bundle. The schema has no diagnostic-only Quantity flag, so volume, surface_area and center_of_mass are intentionally null rather than hard gates around cosmetic geometry. The nominal measurements are preserved in sections 3 and 5. No reference deviation test is declared.

Opening faces in CAD are voids, not material faces: the single top rim bounds two separate mouths and the slanted lower lips bound the outlets.
