# University of Nottingham highly loaded half-journal bearing: reference packet

Transcribed from Layton J., Medina H., Fadhila H., Rothwell B.C., Ambrose S., Farbrother K., Eastwick C. (2026) *Experimental
Investigation of a Highly Loaded Half-Journal Bearing*, Lubricants 14(2), 76, doi:10.3390/lubricants14020076 (CC BY 4.0),
Section 2 (experimental method) only. No measured results are included.

## Unit under test

- A half-journal configuration: an instrumented shaft and a bush that spans 180 degrees of the circumference, contained in a
  chamber at ambient pressure. Both are EN24T steel with an Ra 0.1 surface finish.
- The shaft is supported by two cylindrical roller bearings 230 mm apart.
- The half journal (bush bore) is 100.2 mm in diameter, giving a nominal 100 um radial clearance to the shaft at zero load
  (shaft diameter 100.0 mm). Both are manufactured within a tolerance of 0.01 mm.
- The bearing (bush) is 75 mm long; the axial centreline is at axial position 37.5 mm.
- Oil is supplied through an inlet film generator at the inlet edge of the arc. The supply flow is set high enough to ensure
  hydrodynamic lubrication (20 L/min at the highest speed; some oil overflows into the chamber rather than entering the
  film). The oil tank is maintained at 30 degC by a cartridge heater; oil drains under gravity.
- Angles are measured from bottom dead centre (BDC) of the lubrication region, positive in the direction the shaft surface
  moves: the arc runs from -90 deg (inlet edge, oil feed) through BDC (0 deg) to +90 deg (outlet edge). The applied load
  presses the shaft toward the bush along BDC.

## Lubricant: Eastman Turbo Oil 2197

- Density 996.8 kg/m3 at 15 degC ("0.9968" in the source).
- Kinematic viscosity 26.98 mm2/s at 40 degC and 5.28 mm2/s at 100 degC.
- The specific heat is not stated in the paper.

## Instrumentation

- Six pressure tappings in the bush: at -20 deg and -45 deg from BDC, each at axial positions 18.5, 37.5 (centreline) and
  56 mm. STS 133209 sensors, 0-200 bar; 95 % of the data within +-0.9534 bar.
- Twenty K-type thermocouples embedded 1 mm below the shaft / bush surfaces (class 2, +-2.5 degC); the bush centreline
  row runs from -85 deg (nearest the inlet) to +90 deg. Temperatures are steady time averages.
- Two ultrasonic sensors on the shaft measure the film thickness between -90 and +90 deg from BDC (verified against a
  known gap within 2.1 um).
- Angular position: RLS MR050E magnetic ring (total estimated error 0.22 deg). Shaft speed set point uncertainty +-4 rpm.

## Test matrix

Shaft speed 1000-4000 rpm in 500 rpm steps; applied normal load 5-20 kN in 2.5 kN steps (the complete matrix is the
paper's Figure 6).
