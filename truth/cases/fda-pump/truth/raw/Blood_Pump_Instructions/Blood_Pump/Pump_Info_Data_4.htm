Time Histories for Interlaboratory Study 2: Blood Pump

  • This is for unsteady simulations only.  We would like to quantitatively assess the time histories of some quantities like the inlet and outlet pressures.
  • Please provide a file with the time histories for pump flow rate = 6.0 L/min and pump speed = 3500 rpm only.
  • Please provide the data in an ASCII text file with the following name:

time_histories.txt

  • Please provide data formatted as follows:
    • Data names should go on the first line between quotes, so we will know the order in which the data following is given.

Example:

"time"  "Pinlet"  "Poutlet"  "ShaftTorque"  "MaxWSSrim"  "MaxWSSfillet"

The data need not be in this order, but please make sure the data names are in the same order as the numeric data.

    • On the next lines, please provide the time histories as lines of numbers formatted like this:

t1  Pi1  Po1  ST1  MaxWSSr1  MaxWSSf1

t2  Pi2  Po2  ST2  MaxWSSr2  MaxWSSf2

t3  Pi3  Po3  ST3  MaxWSSr3  MaxWSSf3

  .

  .

  .

tn  Pin  Pon  STn  MaxWSSrn  MaxWSSfn

where:

      • ti is the time in seconds at time i
      • Pii is the pressure averaged over the inlet (N/m2) at time i
      • Poi is the pressure averaged over the outlet (N/m2) at time i
      • STi is the shaft torque at time i in N‑m
      • MaxWSSri is the maximum wall shear stress magnitude (defined here) at time i on the housing rim surface (the half where y < 0, as shown here), in N/m2
      • MaxWSSri is the maximum wall shear stress magnitude (defined here) at time i on the outlet fillet surface, in N/m2.
    • Important note: t1 should correspond to when the rotor blades are 90° to outlet.
