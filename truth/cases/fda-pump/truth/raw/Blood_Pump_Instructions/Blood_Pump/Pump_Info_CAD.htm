CAD Files for Interlaboratory Study 2: Blood Pump

We have provided 3D computer-aided design files for your use in the zipped folder available at the link below.  Because the pump geometry is somewhat complex, we encourage you to use these files.  It is still up to you to create the mesh.

 

  • Included in the zipped file are CAD files in three formats (ACIS, IGES, and STEP), created in GAMBIT (Ansys, Inc.).
  • The housing and rotor are separate 3D CAD surface models.  The housing model represents the inner surfaces of the pump chamber.  You will have to subtract the rotor from the housing to get the proper flow domain.  The rotor is in the proper position relative to the housing, and will give the proper housing/rotor clearance when subtracted.
  • It is up to you to define an interface between stationary and rotating (or rotating frame of reference) domains if needed.
  • Important note: you will have to re-scale the models to use SI units.  The CAD files were created using a scale factor of 1 unit = 100 μm.  You should get a housing diameter of 0.06 m = 6 cm.
  • If you perform steady flow simulations, please do so with one of the rotor blades 90° to the outlet. This is how the CAD files are oriented.
  • The housing files include the curved inlet section (necessary for experimental flow visualization), in case you wish to include it.  The curved section may contribute to secondary flows to the inlet flow to the pump.
  • The 3D files available below were created in Cartesian (x,y,z) coordinates.  See the geometry page for the definition of coordinates.
