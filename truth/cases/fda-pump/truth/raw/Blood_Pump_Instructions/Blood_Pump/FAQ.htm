Questions/Comments

Frequently Asked Questions

  1. Can I use any CFD software?

      Yes.

 

  1. What if I've programmed my own software or subroutines?

      You can use that, too.

 

  1. What turbulence model/boundary conditions/wall functions/ upwinding/relaxation/solver/etc. method should I use?

      All the details of the simulations are up to your discretion.

 

  1. Do I have to calculate blood damage?

It's optional, but encouraged.  We will still include your results in the fluid dynamics analysis.

 

  1. I only have time/money/resources to do a few of the simulations.  Is that OK?

Sure.  Send us results from the simulations you finish – but please, only send complete data sets for each simulation that you submit.

 

  1. How will the data be blinded?

Submitted data will be stripped of identifying information (i.e. participant’s name and institution) and given a code by an FDA employee not directly associated with the project.  We will then analyze the data with the coded header.

 

  1. If the data will be blinded, then why send name and institution information at all?

The same uninvolved person will retain a database identifying the codes.  If there is a problem with the data, we can then go back through that person to contact you.  This is the only way this information will be used.

 

  1. Who's going to analyze the data?

The Project's Technical Steering Committee will be analyzing the blinded data.

 

  1. Can I send my data in my own chosen format?

Please only send data in format we've described.  We plan to use automated scripts to extract and analyze the data

 

  1. Is the data going to be published?

Yes, we want to disseminate the results as widely as possible. However, only the blinded data will be published without identifying who submitted it.

 

  1. Can I be an author on the papers you publish?

We will acknowledge the participants en masse, but because we want to have as many participants as possible, we can't put everyone on the "authors" list.  However, if you want to do additional studies based on this work, you are free to do so -- in fact, we encourage it.  We would like these models to become de facto standards of CFD blood damage modeling.

 

  1. What other deliverables will come from the project?

Based on this study and others like it, we plan to write an FDA Guidance Document on the use of CFD in applying for regulatory approval of blood-contacting medical devices.  We also hope to provide input into a standard being developed by the Association for the Advancement of Medical Instrumentation.  Finally, we hope to provide information on critically-needed areas of research in the field.

 

  1. Will I retain ownership of the results from my CFD modelling?

Yes, the individual data sets are owned by the person who did the computations.  Once the data are submitted to us, the FDA can use it any way that it wants in order to fulfill the goals of this study.  However, we will never identify anyone who submitted a particular data set.

 

  1. Once the results are available in the literature (along with the experimental results), can I present it with my data to client/conferences/public events/marketing/case studies/other?

Once the amalgamated data is published, it can be used like data from any other published study with proper reference.  However, as always, you cannot say or imply that the FDA sanctions your work.

 

  1. Will I receive recognition for participating in the project?

Yes, the names and affiliations of individuals who participate in this project will be listed on the Participant Appreciation page of the project’s internet website at https://fdacfd.nci.nih.gov.
