Submit Your Data for Interlaboratory Study 2: Blood Pump

Please submit your data by July 31, 2014.  If you have any problem meeting this deadline, please let us know.

 

Once you have signed up, and have completed your studies (and created data and image files in the proper data format), compress all your data files into one *.zip or *.tar file.  Encrypt your file with a password of your choice.  The *.zip or *.tar file filename should be some unique, non-identifiable name.  For example:

   Potassium_Chloride.zip

   Gone_With_The_Wind.zip

   Forever_Yours.tar

Then transfer the zipped file to our anonymous ftp server:

 

  1. ftp to ost.cdrh.fda.gov.
  2. Use the login and password sent to you when you signed up.
  3. If needed, issue the BINARY command (because the compressed file you will upload is binary).
  4. Use the PUT command to transfer the compressed file to our server.
  5. Quit from your ftp client.
  6. Notify us that your submission is on the ftp server.  In that email, also provide us with the following:

·       Name of your compressed file.

·       Your encryption password.

·       Any further suggestions or comments you have.

This two part submission process should be sufficient to protect the contents of your file.  We will remove the file from the server as soon as possible.

 

Thank you for your interest and participation!
