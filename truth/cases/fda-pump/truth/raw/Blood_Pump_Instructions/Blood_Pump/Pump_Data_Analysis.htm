Planned Data Analysis for Interlaboratory Study 2: Blood Pump

We plan to do the following analyses:

 

  • Compare pump performance data (e.g., HQ curves, pump efficiencies) from the simulations and experiments.  This will provide a snapshot of how accurately CFD can predict global measures.
  • Compare velocities, pressures, shear stresses, and eddy viscosities along planes and surfaces from the simulations and flow visualization experiments.  We also will quantitatively compare points and lines of interest extracted from the planes and surfaces.  This will give insight into the ability of CFD to predict local phenomena.
  • Compare hemolysis indices computed from simulations with those from the blood damage experiments.  This will provide knowledge on the ability of CFD to predict the biology from the physics.
  • Compare inlet and outlet pressures, shaft torque, and cutwater wall shear stress time histories from unsteady simulations.  This will give a picture of how the solutions evolve over time.
  • Analyze the above with respect to simulation parameters.  We hope this will give some insight into CFD best practices.
  • Please let us know if you have comments or suggestions.
