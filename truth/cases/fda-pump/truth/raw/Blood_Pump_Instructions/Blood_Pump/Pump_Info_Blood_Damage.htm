Estimate of Blood Damage for Interlaboratory Study 2: Blood Pump

  • An important goal for applying CFD simulations is to predict blood damage caused by medical devices.
  • For comparative purposes, participants can choose to provide estimates of hemolysis for each of the 6 pump operating conditions.
  • As there are several methods available to predict hemolysis, please provide information on the model that you used (or a published reference) in the simulation parameters spreadsheet.
  • Please report a Relative Index of Hemolysis (RIH), defined as the ratio to the value you calculate at 6.0 L/min and 3500 RPM (to three significant digits).  Thus the RIH at 6.0 L/min and 3500 RPM should be set equal to 1.00. This will allow us to compare indices regardless of method.
  • Please also provide your absolute measure of hemolysis (i.e., generated plasma hemoglobin concentration in units of mg/dl), to three significant digits.  This will allow us to compare results that used the same method (if possible).
  • The following information may or may not be necessary for your calculations:

Blood Species

porcine

25° C

0.0035 N-s/m2

  • Note: All blood-contacting surfaces of the blood pump model and fabricated flow loop components were polished, and have average surface roughness (Ra) values below 0.6 μm.
  • Note: Although it was necessary to use a mechanical shaft seal (made of PTFE) in the blood pump experiments, a recessed seal design was used to minimize hemolysis caused by the seal.
