Model Information for Interlaboratory Study 2: Blood Pump

The subject of Interlaboratory Study 2 is a simplified centrifugal blood pump. Below is an image of the the pump chamber and rotor (click on image to see larger version):

 

 

Please see the geometry page for the dimensions and details.  We have built acrylic versions of these pumps, and are running particle image velocimetry experiments to characterize the flow fields in the pump.  We are also running blood damage experiments, as well as pump performance (H-Q) tests.  A schematic representation of both experimental setups is available here.

To participate in the Interlaboratory Study:

  • Sign up for the study.
  • Create the pump geometry.  We recommend that you download and use a set of our neutral CAD files, due to the complexity of some of the pump surfaces.  Alternatively, you can create the above geometry in your own CAD program.
  • Mesh the flow domain to your own taste.
  • Run CFD simulations at specified flow rates and pump speeds.
  • Extract pump performance data from the CFD solutions.
  • Interpolate velocity, pressure, and shear stress data from your CFD solutions onto pre-defined cross-sections and surfaces.
  • For unsteady simulations only, create time histories of some of the numeric measures.
  • Optionally, calculate an index of blood damage for each flow rate and RPM.
  • Create images of the meshes you used in your computations.
  • Format your results as specified in this document.
  • Submit your results to us.

We plan to analyze the data by comparing the CFD results among participants (in a blinded fashion), and by comparing them to the experimental data.  We will then present the results in publications and presentations, and post all data and documentation at our website, https://fdacfd.nci.nih.gov.

Thank you for collaborating with us!
