Introduction

  • The test object is a simplified version of a centrifugal blood pump.
  • CAD files of the pump housing chamber and the rotor are available here.  We recommend you use the CAD files.  Let us know if you have problems loading them.

Pump Rotor

  • The pump rotor has a complex geometry, in part due to polishing.
  • The pump rotor has an outside diameter of 0.052 m.  It has four blades situated at 90° angles. The rotor blades are each 0.003 m by 0.003 m by 0.0185 m long.  Including the blades, the rotor is 0.007 m thick.
  • The rotor shaft diameter is 0.0032 m.
  • Below is an image of the CAD model, and a drawing showing dimensions.  Click on a figure to see it larger.

  • After machining, the experimental acrylic rotors were hand-polished to specifications.  The rotor edges are filleted, with radii as shown below.  Measurements of the rotor fillet radii using microphotography and metrology compound were made (note: some of the edges were not measured due to difficulties in visualization, but were estimated).
  • The CAD files we supply include these fillets (but how finely you mesh the fillets is up to you).

Pump Housing

  • The inner diameter of the pump housing chamber is 0.06 m.  The inner width of the housing chamber is 0.009 m.
  • The drawings below show the inside surfaces of the pump housing and tubing only.  The outer surfaces of the pump housing and tubing are not shown.  The drawings with dimensions are not to scale.  Click on a figure to see it larger.

 

  • The cutwater is identified in yellow in the image below, and shown magnified in the circle.  The acrylic model used for experiments has a minimum measured fillet radius of 0.00004 m (40 μm) along the cutwater.  The CAD files we supply include this fillet (meshing it, however, is up to you).  The drawing with dimensions on the right is not to scale.  Click on a figure to see it larger.

Housing/Rotor Clearances

  • The clearances between the housing and the front and back rotor faces are equal to 0.001 m.  The clearance between the housing and the rotor rim is 0.004 m. The drawing with dimensions on the right below is not to scale.  Click on a figure to see it larger.

Inlet

  • The inlet tube has an inner diameter of 0.012 m.
  • The inlet tube is curved with a 0.09 m radius to accommodate the flow visualization experiments, and leads into a straight inlet tube that extends 0.204 m from the end of the curved tube to the opposite side of the housing chamber.  There is a 0.005 m fillet where the inlet tube meets the pump chamber.  Due to secondary flows in the curved inlet tube, the entrance velocity profile to the pump may be skewed.  The drawing on the right is not to scale.

Outlet

  • The outlet throat is 0.02 m long, with a 0.00439 m inner diameter.
  • The throat widens via a 0.02154 m long diffuser, to the 0.012 m inner diameter outlet tube.
  • The configuration of any extension to the outlet is left to your discretion.
