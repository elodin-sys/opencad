Experimental Setup for Interlaboratory Study 2: Blood Pump

  • The experimental setup (flow loop dimensions, transducer positions) for the blood damage experiments and the flow visualization experiments are similar.  The main difference is the fluid used (blood vs. NaI/water/glycerin), and the flow rates/pump speed in the flow visualization experiments (which were scaled to reflect the difference in fluid density and viscosity).
  • Below is a schematic diagram of the experimental setup (click on image to see a larger version), showing the position used to measure velocities at the entrance to the curved inlet tube.
  • The inlet pressure probe is at the same height as the pump axis, and 0.175 m from the bottom of the pump chamber. The outlet pressure probe is 0.245 m above the pump axis.
  • The schematic below is not to scale.

  • Axial velocities were measured at the entrance to the curved inlet tube by particle image velocimetry.

  • The representative velocity profiles are given in the Simulation Conditions section.
