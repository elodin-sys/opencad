  • Axial velocities and inlet turbulence intensities at the inlet to the curved tube were measured experimentally by particle image velocimetry.

  • The mean velocity profiles are shown in the figures below.  A spreadsheet with the measured and mean velocities (from three individual experiments) is available here.  These profiles are provided as a guide to developing your inlet velocity boundary conditions.  (For inlet turbulence intensity boundary conditions, see the table above.)  The intended flow rates are given above the figures. Because the radial positions varied slightly among the separate PIV experiments, linear interpolation was used to calculate the mean velocity profiles.  Note: using these velocity profiles directly as inlet boundary conditions may require some scaling, due to small experimental errors in the measurements.
