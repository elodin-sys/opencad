Surfaces

  • Please extract wall shear stress data and pressure data on the surfaces defined below, and return the formatted data in ASCII text files.
  • Please provide data over all the surfaces described below for pump flow rate = 6.0 L/min and pump speed = 3500 RPM only.
  • Please provide the data with one of the rotor blades 90° to the outlet (closest to the last time step if the simulation is performed with unsteady flow).
  • Surfaces are identified by the blood pump part:
    • Surface 1 is the throat wall.  The surface is a cylinder, truncated by the intersection with the outlet fillet.
    • Surface 2 is the outlet diffuser wall.  The surface is a truncated cone.
    • Surface 3 is a 180° section of the rim of the housing chamber surrounding the outlet.  The surface is a section of a cylinder, except for the hole defined by the intersection with the throat & outlet fillet.
    • Surface 4 is the outlet fillet, making up the cutwater.
    • Surface 5 is the top surface of the rotor, including blades and rotor rim
  • Click on the individual image for a larger view.
